# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 21, 2026

Here is what this Discord bot does with information when you use it:

- **What it reads:** When handling sign-ups, lists, or closed tickets, the bot can read things like Discord IDs, team names, role colors, regions, and the details you put in your application.
- **Why it uses them:** Only to give out roles, put teams in the right groups/regions, and keep track of entries for the server or event.
- **Where it goes:** Your info isn't sold or handed out to random third parties. It may stay in local files, logs, or lists used by the organizers to run things and fix issues.
- **Removing info:** If you want your entry or details checked or taken off tournament lists, just message the server staff or event organizers.
